My story

Building toward something bigger.

I'm a senior at Taylor Allderdice High School in Pittsburgh, and I've been taking college courses through CCAC's BNY Early College program since February 2025. I like being challenged, and I believe there's always something to learn.

I speak three languages, and a lot of my work happens where those worlds meet. With ARYSE, I help design programs for refugee and immigrant youth and reach out to local refugee families. Through Valore Group, I connect refugee and immigrant professionals with meaningful work.

I also build. Through Halvora, I make websites and AI tools for businesses and nonprofits, and I teach STEM at Carnegie Mellon. My goal is clear: build a business that gives me financial freedom and the means to help the people and communities that helped shape me.

"I don't see refugees — I see doctors, artists, engineers, and leaders waiting for the world to catch up." Rechab, Founder of Valore Group
